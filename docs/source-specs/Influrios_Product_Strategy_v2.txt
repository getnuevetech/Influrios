1. Executive Summary
The product should not be positioned as another influencer directory. The stronger opportunity is to build a searchable influence network that helps brands discover the right creators, helps creators collaborate with complementary creators, and gives every influencer a portable commercial identity that brings all of their social presence into one place.
The platform has four connected engines:
Influence Discovery
A free-to-access directory organized around what a creator actually influences, not only follower count or a self-selected broad category.
Creator Collaboration Network
A matching layer where influencers can find complementary specialists and jointly serve a customer, brand, campaign or project.
Influence Card + Share Identity
A portable digital creator identity that aggregates social presence, specialties, location, commercial information and contact actions. Starter launches with a shareable profile link; QR and shortlink capabilities are added according to Plus/Pro entitlements.
Business Matching + Managed Promotion
A monetized layer that introduces influencers to relevant businesses, matches businesses to creators, supports outreach and can facilitate longer-term commercial relationships.
Core thesis: make discovery free enough to create traffic and network density, then monetize intelligence, access, matching, workflow and managed commercial services.

2. Product Positioning
Recommended category: Influence Discovery & Collaboration Platform.
Simple promise: “Find the right influence, connect the right people, and turn reach into business relationships.”
Brand architecture
Platform brand: Influrios. The creator identity product may be presented publicly as “Influrios Card” or “Influencer Card by Influrios,” while “Influencer Card” remains the descriptive product category. The approved brand meaning is intentionally coined and global rather than tied to one language or geography. Working brand line: “Influence. Identity. Opportunity.”
Naming rule: product copy, transactional messages, QR landing experiences, card exports and admin labels should use Influrios consistently. Do not revive the former Influence Connect working name in new production assets.
The problem
Influencers are fragmented across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, LinkedIn and emerging platforms; a business often sees only one account at a time.
Existing discovery tools frequently lead with follower counts and broad categories, which can hide the creator’s real specialty and practical commercial value.
Creators who could strengthen one another often have no structured way to discover complementary collaborators.
Small and mid-sized businesses may know the type of audience they want but do not know which creators are relevant, reachable, available or commercially appropriate.
Many creators are good at content but weak at packaging themselves for business development, outreach and long-term partnerships.
The solution
Create a public influence graph that connects three kinds of information:
What the influencer is known for and can credibly promote or demonstrate.
Who the influencer can collaborate with to create a better combined offering.
Which businesses, audiences and commercial opportunities fit that influence profile.

6. Influencer-to-Influencer Collaboration Network
This is one of the strongest differentiators. The platform should let creators describe both what they can contribute and what they need from another specialist. Matching then becomes complementary rather than competitive.
The collaboration model
1. Declare  Influencer publishes collaboration offers, needs, location, availability and preferred project types.
2. Match  System recommends complementary creators based on specialty, audience adjacency, geography, platform fit and project goal.
3. Propose  One creator sends a structured collaboration proposal or a business can invite a pre-built creator team.
4. Build  Participants agree scope, content, roles, commercial terms and customer ownership.
5. Showcase  Completed work can become a joint portfolio case study, strengthening both creator profiles.

7. Matching & Recommendation Logic
The platform should support two different matching problems: creator-to-creator collaboration and business-to-creator fit. They should use separate logic and explain why a match was suggested.

Matching should be explainable. “Why this match?” becomes a trust feature and prevents the product from feeling like a black-box ranking system.

9. Business Matching & Managed Influencer Marketing
This is the highest-value monetization layer. The platform can do more than let a business search: it can actively introduce creators to business categories that fit their specialty and help turn introductions into ongoing relationships.
Managed creator promotion workflow
Opt in: Influencer selects the industries, business types, geography and partnership formats they want.
Package: Platform builds or reviews the influencer’s commercial profile, media kit, case studies and positioning.
Target: Platform identifies relevant businesses and buyer roles using the creator’s specialty and audience fit.
Introduce: Platform sends permissioned outreach / introductions and routes interest back through the platform.
Facilitate: Platform helps structure the brief, deliverables, timeline and commercial discussion.
Retain: Successful one-off campaigns can be converted into recurring brand relationships or ambassador arrangements.
Business-side managed matching
Business submits a campaign/customer need rather than searching manually.
Platform recommends individual creators or a complementary creator team.
Business receives a short, reasoned shortlist rather than hundreds of profiles.
Optional platform support covers outreach, scheduling, brief alignment and relationship administration.
10. Monetization Architecture
The free product should create density and traffic. Paid products should monetize deeper information, workflow, access and human-assisted commercial outcomes.

Do not sell “verification” as credibility. Verification should be earned through identity/account checks. Premium users can pay for tools and promotion, but sponsored placement must be clearly labeled and should not overwrite relevance-based search.

12. Core Data Model
The data model should be designed around relationships, not simply profiles. Each creator is a node connected to specialties, social channels, audiences, businesses, collaborations and opportunities.

13. Trust, Data & Commercial Safeguards
The platform’s long-term value depends on trusted data and legitimate outreach. These rules should be part of the product design from the beginning.
Use official APIs, creator authorization and other permitted data sources rather than designing the business around uncontrolled scraping.
Provide a clear claim, correction and opt-out process for creator profiles created from public information.
Separate creator-supplied claims from platform-verified fields and show data freshness.
Do not expose personal email/phone details without creator consent; use platform inquiry routing where appropriate.
Clearly label sponsored placement and paid promotion.
Require campaign disclosures and support compliance with applicable advertising/endorsement rules.
Create creator/business terms covering usage rights, deliverables, cancellation, payments and dispute procedures before facilitating deals.
If the platform markets a creator on their behalf, obtain explicit authorization defining scope, compensation and whether the relationship is exclusive or non-exclusive.
Design abuse controls for impersonation, fake metrics, spam, prohibited categories and repeated bad actors.
14. MVP Scope & Development Sequence
The first release should prove discovery, identity and collaboration before becoming a full campaign marketplace.

Recommended MVP rule: do not begin with escrow, creator payouts, full contracts, campaign accounting or complex social publishing. First prove that people want the discovery graph, the Influence Card and the collaboration/matching layer.

16. Growth Loops
Influence Card loop: Creator shares QR/profile → new people visit platform → more businesses/creators discover directory → more profiles claimed.
Collaboration loop: Creator finds partner → joint content tags both creators + platform profile → shared audience creates new platform traffic.
Business proof loop: Successful collaboration becomes case study → strengthens creator profile and platform credibility → attracts more business briefs.
SEO / discovery loop: Public specialty/location pages answer long-tail searches → organic visitors enter directory → creators have an incentive to claim and improve profiles.
Relationship loop: One-off introduction becomes recurring partnership → platform retains both sides through workflow, analytics and relationship history.

18. Recommended Product Definition
The product should be built around one connected identity: an influencer is not merely a social account; they are a searchable body of influence, a potential collaborator, and a commercial relationship partner.
The platform’s initial identity can therefore be summarized as:
A free influence discovery network where creators bring all of their social presence into one professional profile, discover complementary collaborators, and become reachable by the businesses that need their specific influence. Premium intelligence, workflow, matching and managed business development create the monetization layers.
Recommended launch priorities
1. Define the specialty taxonomy and creator profile schema.
2. Build claimable public profiles and the tier-aware Influencer Card system: Starter link, Plus standard QR, Pro dynamic QR.
3. Launch free search with strong specialty + geography filters.
4. Add “I offer / I need” collaboration matching.
5. Add business briefs, shortlists and qualified inquiries.
6. Test Creator Pro and Business Pro subscriptions.
7. Manually pilot managed creator promotion and business matching before automating it.
8. Only after the above works, consider campaign contracting, payments or broader marketplace infrastructure.

19. Influencer Card as a Standalone Product & Monetization System
The Influencer Card should be treated as a monetizable product in its own right, not merely a visual component of the creator profile. A creator may subscribe to the card product even if they do not actively use the wider marketplace. This makes the card an acquisition funnel into discovery, collaboration and business-matching services.
Canonical product distinction: Influencer Card = portable identity and connection. Full Influencer Profile = discovery, intelligence, portfolio, audience and commercial depth.
Vertical card standard
The canonical Influencer Card is portrait/vertical. Horizontal compositions may be used only as promotional previews or website modules; they are not the card standard.
The shared visual reference uses an approximately 4:5 portrait canvas. Production should be responsive rather than fixed to raster dimensions.
Card content is live data. Social links, QR, contact actions, name, specialties, statistics and brand fields must remain interactive UI components.
The creator photo and optional background art can be raster assets; card structure, text, controls and QR should be generated/rendered dynamically.
Starter / Plus / Pro card feature model

Commercial rule: the values above are launch defaults, not hard-coded product rules. Admin must be able to change limits and enabled features per plan, country, promotion or bundle without a deployment.
QR and link strategy
Starter: canonical Influrios profile/card URL; no QR in the launch default.
Plus: shortlink (for example ic.me/sofia) plus a standard QR that resolves to the card URL. The QR is useful for sharing but is not independently retargetable.
Pro: dynamic QR using a platform-controlled opaque redirect identity (for example ic.me/q/7Hd92K). The creator can update social links, contact methods, portfolio and other card content without replacing printed QR artwork.
Pro QR analytics can include scan count, timestamp, source/campaign, device class and coarse geography where privacy and law permit. Exact precise location is unnecessary.
Standalone card purchase and upsell path
The card can be sold independently or bundled with creator-platform subscriptions. A creator may begin with the card for networking and later upgrade into marketplace intelligence, collaboration matching and managed promotion. Conversely, creator-platform plans may include a card tier as a bundle entitlement. Admin controls bundle relationships and prices.
Brand color system
Influrios uses blue for trust/platform behavior and purple for creator identity/collaboration. Pink is an accent, not a dominant category signal. Champagne gold is reserved for Pro-level premium accents. This avoids making the platform feel beauty-specific and keeps the identity suitable for technology, fitness, automotive, food, travel, interiors, gaming, finance, suppliers and other creator categories.

20. Influencer Onboarding, Acquisition & Activation Strategy
Primary onboarding principle: show the influencer value before asking for a full registration. The strongest creator acquisition message is not “join another influencer marketplace”; it is “create your free Influencer Card — one professional identity for your creator presence.”
Recommended creator acquisition wedge: “Create Your Free Influencer Card. One Card. All Your Influence.”
Onboarding experience: value before registration
1. Start with one social profile URL, handle or creator name. Do not require a full account form before showing value.
2. Generate a private draft card/profile preview from permitted public data and/or connected provider data. Clearly label inferred data as a draft.
3. Show the creator the result, then ask them to claim it. The claim action is the natural point to require registration and consent.
4. Verify identity/control of at least one appropriate channel. Keep identity verification, connected-social verification and data verification as separate statuses.
5. Use AI to propose specialties, creator title, summary and other low-risk profile fields; let the creator confirm, edit or remove the suggestions.
6. Publish the effective Starter card/profile immediately after required verification and consent. Give the creator a real shareable asset on day one.
7. Continue setup progressively through profile-completeness prompts instead of a long mandatory onboarding form.

Upgrade behavior should be contextual
The platform should not force creators into a pricing page before they experience value. When a creator attempts a locked capability, explain the benefit and the entitlement required. The UI must use effective entitlements, not hard-coded plan-name rules.
Acquisition attribution and funnel reporting

Preserve first-touch and last-touch attribution, invitation/campaign identifiers and referrer where legally/technically available.
Admin should report Invited → Previewed → Claim Started → Verified → Published → Shared → Engaged → Plus/Pro conversion by source/campaign/category/country.
Track quality, not only registrations: active cards, completeness, shares, business inquiries, collaboration activity, retention and paid conversion by source.
