Projection (DB1 · finding 4)

  1. Invitations card shows open invitations (3) with the waiting for your reply subset (1). Home shows only the subset, so the two screens agree.
  2. Team invitations are listed here like marketplace invitations (P1-AUD-04, found in the IPS-44 run): pending ones count as waiting; accepted-awaiting-teammates rows stay listed with “Waiting for teammates”.
  3. Tab badges: Invitations 1 (waiting for your reply) + Active 2 (Spring Launch Reel Milestones 1 and 2). The Collaborations sidebar badge is their sum, 3, so it counts the invitation and the content tasks.
  4. Studio Tour Shorts' signature task is counted on Contracts (1), not here; the Active stat names it (“1 to sign”) so the row is explained.
  5. Mobile keeps all five tabs (Overview · Invitations · Applications · Active · Completed), wrapping instead of dropping any, and every row keeps its Open / Review button.
  6. Question sent and Interested rows stay listed with a status naming who acts next. They drop out of the badge until the business replies.
  7. Empty copy “No invitations are waiting” appears only when open invitations = 0.
