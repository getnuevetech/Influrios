MOB1

  1. Fixed nav bar: 3 most used destinations + More in four equal slots. It never scrolls horizontally.
  2. More carries the sum of badges for destinations not in the bar. Influencer example: Contracts 1 → More 1 (Earnings is in the bar). Business: Contracts is in the bar.
  3. Highlight rule: a destination in the bar highlights its tab. A destination that lives in the drawer (Action inbox, Contracts, Messages…) highlights More. Notifications is reached from the bell and highlights nothing. Dashboard is highlighted only on Dashboard.
  4. Drawer lists every sidebar destination in the same order, labels, icons and badges as desktop, including Billing & Plans and Collaboration Payments.
  5. Business label is “Discover Influencers”.
  6. What these frames don't prove: static 320, 390 and 430 px frames show layout at three widths only. The full 320–430 px range, keyboard behaviour (Tab order, Esc closes the drawer, focus trapped while open and returned to More on close), aria-expanded, screen-reader names and zoom are runtime acceptance for MOB1 in S-slice testing, not settled by these images.
