SYSTEM OVERVIEW
1. Executive Architecture
What Dev AI is building and the rules it must not violate.
1.1 Product definition
Influrios Collaboration is a global collaboration operating system connecting creators, businesses, agencies and complementary creators. It must support public discovery, signed-in matching, business requests, creator opportunities, business collaboration suggestions, collaboration contracting, full funding, milestone-controlled release, commissions, creator payout readiness, multi-country payment routing, disputes, analytics and mentorship-led onboarding.
1.2 Core domain modules


1.3 Non-negotiable invariants
Collaboration-funded money must never settle into or be treated as Influrios/Nueve operating cash before the corresponding platform fee is earned.
Creator funds must never route through the Influrios Operations Account.
Influrios Operations / Business Account and Collaboration Holding / Protected Funds must be distinct financial domains in both the provider configuration and internal ledger.
A collaboration may not become fundable until a valid payment route and creator payout route are known.
A creator may not be marked Global Payout Ready until identity/KYC and at least one supported payout route are verified.
Milestone percentages plus any non-milestone allocation must equal exactly 100% of the collaboration amount; rounding must be deterministic.
The platform fee/commission is calculated once from the deal snapshot, then earned and released according to milestone rules; an Admin fee change must never retroactively alter an existing contract.
Payment providers execute money movement. Influrios remains the source of truth for contract state, milestone state and business authorization to release.
No Airwallex-, Flutterwave-, Escrow.com- or other provider-specific business logic may be hard-coded into Collaboration domain rules.
All release, refund, dispute and payout requests must be idempotent and auditable.

6. Milestone System
Milestones are product logic first, payment instructions second.
6.1 Milestone templates controlled by Admin
Admin-defined standard milestones are the default and are recommended. Business-custom milestones are allowed only when the business plan/entitlement enables them.


6.2 Plan entitlement rules


6.3 Milestone validation
Percentages must sum to 100.0000% before funding.
Admin sets minimum and maximum milestone percentage and maximum count.
Currency amounts are derived from the contract amount with deterministic rounding; final milestone absorbs approved rounding remainder.
Creator must approve any custom milestone schedule before contract acceptance.
Once fully signed and funded, percentage/amount edits require a formal change order; never overwrite the original schedule.
Each milestone stores gross allocation, creator allocation, platform fee allocation, provider references and release state.
6.4 Milestone lifecycle
DRAFT → FUNDED → ACTIVE → SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW → APPROVED → RELEASE_AUTHORIZED → PROVIDER_RELEASE_REQUESTED → RELEASE_CONFIRMED → CREATOR_AVAILABLE.
Exception states: REVISION_REQUESTED, DISPUTED, CANCELLED, REFUND_PENDING, REFUNDED, COMPLIANCE_HOLD, PAYMENT_ERROR.
FINANCIAL SYSTEM
7. Collaboration Finance Architecture
Clean separation of company money, protected collaboration money and creator earnings.
7.1 Financial domains




7.2 Contract funding model
Example: $10,000 collaboration, 7.5% platform fee, Standard 20/30/30/20 milestones.


At funding, the full $10,000 belongs to the collaboration financial domain. Influrios Operations receives $0. The $750 is recorded as maximum allocated platform fee but remains unearned until milestones are approved.
7.3 Release behavior
When M1 is approved, the system authorizes two releases from the collaboration financial domain: $1,850 to the creator-connected financial destination and $150 of earned platform fee to Influrios Operations. The remaining $8,000 stays protected.
Creator release and platform-fee release must be linked to the same milestone event but may have separate provider transaction references.
If the provider cannot atomically release both legs, Influrios records a compound release transaction and reconciles both legs independently.
A platform fee is not EARNED until the underlying milestone meets the contract release condition.
Cancellation after partial completion releases/refunds only according to the immutable financial schedule and cancellation rules.
7.4 Fee configuration
Commission must be Admin-configurable and versioned. Never hard-code 7.5%.


PAYMENTS
8. Payment Orchestration & Provider Abstraction
Airwallex may be primary, but Collaboration must remain gateway-independent.
8.1 Provider adapter contract
Implement a PaymentProviderAdapter interface. Collaboration domain calls business capabilities, never provider endpoints directly.


8.2 Airwallex assumed integration model
Current official Airwallex documentation supports connected accounts, multiple FundsSplit records on a payment, manual-release split funds, PSP-agnostic Holding Accounts, platform-fee splits, Global Accounts and multiple accounts for one legal entity. These capabilities make Airwallex a strong candidate for the primary implementation, but production behavior must be validated in sandbox and under Influrios' actual approved program.
Preferred conceptual structure: Influrios Operations Account is separate from Collaboration Holding / protected marketplace flow.
Creators are onboarded as connected accounts where the approved country/account model permits.
A fully funded collaboration generates provider-side milestone allocations/references with manual release.
Milestone approval in Influrios triggers provider release; provider webhooks confirm actual movement.
Earned fee may be moved from Holding to the platform wallet/Operations according to the approved Airwallex program.
Global Accounts are receiving rails, not substitutes for the internal separation between Operations and Collaboration.
Do not assume any country/account/payout capability until the Airwallex program configuration confirms it.
8.3 Mandatory Airwallex validation items
Confirm multiple manual-release FundsSplit objects from one funded collaboration can target the same creator connected account when representing multiple milestones.
Confirm the exact Holding Account / connected-account topology for collaboration funds while keeping Nueve/Influrios Operations separate.
Confirm the cleanest way to keep platform fee allocations unearned/restricted until the corresponding milestone is approved.
Confirm supported connected-account types by creator country: full, withdrawal, ledger, individual/business.
Confirm Global Account availability and supported receiving currencies for target countries.
Confirm payout destinations and FX capabilities by corridor.
Confirm provider-level refund, chargeback, reserve and dispute behavior.
Confirm whether regional payout providers can be used after release without routing creator money through Influrios Operations.
8.4 Other providers
Flutterwave, M-Pesa, Escrow.com, Adyen, Routefusion and future providers are adapters/rails, not core collaboration logic. Admin determines which capabilities are enabled by country and corridor.

RISK CONTROLS
12. Contracts, Reviews, Disputes & Changes
Commercial state and money state must stay synchronized.
12.1 Review / auto-approval
Each milestone has an agreed review window. The business may approve, request a permitted revision, or open a dispute with a structured reason. If no action occurs by the deadline and the contract enables auto-approval, Influrios automatically authorizes release.
Admin default review window; higher business plans may propose custom window if entitled.
Revision count and allowed scope are contract fields.
Auto-approval scheduler must be idempotent and suppress release when a dispute/compliance hold exists.
All approvals/rejections record actor, timestamp, evidence and contract version.
12.2 Change order
After signature/funding, milestone amount or scope changes require a new versioned Change Order. A change order may require additional funding, partial refund or reallocation. Never mutate the original contract schedule silently.
12.3 Disputes
Opening a dispute freezes only the affected milestone amount unless rules require a broader hold.
Evidence supports file/message references, timestamps and deliverable versions.
Admin/approved resolver may decide release-to-creator, refund-to-business, split allocation or negotiated settlement.
Provider instructions are generated only after the dispute decision is committed.
Legal/compliance holds supersede ordinary auto-release.

ENGINEERING
14. Events, Webhooks & Idempotency
Financial correctness depends on event discipline.
14.1 Event-driven model
Provider webhooks update external facts; they must never directly advance commercial milestones without Collaboration Engine validation.


14.2 Idempotency rules
Every provider command uses a stable idempotency key derived from internal transaction ID.
Webhook event IDs are persisted; duplicate events are acknowledged but not re-applied.
A release cannot execute twice even if Admin/user/API retries.
Provider status is reconciled periodically against internal expected state.
Any divergence creates a reconciliation exception, not an automatic money movement.

CONTROLS
17. Security, Audit & Financial Controls
The collaboration system handles high-value, cross-border transactions and must be treated as a financial-grade workflow.
RBAC separates ordinary support, collaboration operations, finance operations, dispute resolution, legal/compliance and super-admin.
No API secret or full bank/payout credential is stored/displayed in plaintext.
Sensitive payout data is tokenized/provider-referenced where possible.
Manual release/refund overrides above threshold require dual approval.
Every money-affecting action includes actor, reason, source IP/session, before/after state and external reference.
High-risk changes (commission rule, account mapping, corridor activation, provider credentials) require elevated permission and audit event.
Creator and business account suspension immediately blocks new contract/funding actions; existing funds follow compliance/provider/legal handling rules.
Provider health monitoring can disable new funding while allowing safe completion/refund of existing deals.

A dispute freezes the affected milestone and blocks auto-release.
Admin can suspend a country/provider route without code deployment.
Airwallex-specific code exists only inside provider implementation/adapters, not domain rules.
All financial actions are fully auditable and reconcilable.
