6. Subscription, Billing, Cancellation & Refund Terms
1. Plans and Pricing
Subscription prices, billing periods, entitlements, taxes, and promotional terms are displayed at checkout and may vary by
country, currency, account type, or plan. The checkout disclosure controls the purchased term unless a signed order form says
otherwise.

2. Automatic Renewal
If a subscription is sold on an automatically renewing basis, Influrios will disclose the recurring nature, amount/frequency, and
cancellation method before purchase as required by applicable law. By accepting the recurring subscription, you authorize
recurring charges until cancellation becomes effective.

3. Upgrades and Downgrades
Upgrades may take effect immediately or at the next billing period as disclosed. Downgrades may take effect at the end of the
current paid period unless law or the checkout flow provides otherwise. Loss of an entitlement may disable future use of a
feature without deleting historical records that Influrios must retain.

4. Cancellation
You may cancel through the account control provided for the subscription or other legally required method. Cancellation stops
future renewal but does not automatically reverse completed charges. Influrios should not require materially more burdensome
cancellation steps than the purchase channel permits.

5. Refunds
Except where law, a promotional guarantee, or a specific order form provides otherwise, subscription fees are generally nonrefundable after the service period begins. Influrios may issue discretionary credits. Collaboration funds, advertising orders,
and managed-service fees follow their separate terms.

6. Failed Payments
Influrios may retry failed charges, suspend paid features, downgrade access, or terminate a paid plan after reasonable notice.
Failed subscription payment does not alter a collaboration payment obligation already accepted under a separate collaboration
agreement.

7. Taxes
Prices may exclude applicable sales, use, VAT, GST, or similar taxes unless checkout states otherwise. Users are responsible for
taxes not collected by Influrios.
