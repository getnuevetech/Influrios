5. AI & Automated Processing Policy
1. Uses
•

Profile extraction and specialty classification

•

Search, ranking, and explainable matching

•

Drafting outreach or profile copy

•

Content moderation and abuse/fraud signals

•

Support assistance and operational summaries

•

Analytics and quality review

2. Human Responsibility
AI output is assistive. Users remain responsible for business decisions, campaign claims, contracts, tax/legal obligations, and
content they publish. Influrios should not present AI-generated legal, nancial, medical, or other regulated advice as
professional advice.

3. Creator Corrections
Creators should be able to confirm, edit, or dispute creator-facing specialty classifications and material profile inferences.
Influrios may maintain internal fraud/security signals that cannot be fully disclosed where disclosure would compromise
security or legal obligations.

4. Sensitive Traits
Influrios should not infer or rank creators by sensitive characteristics such as health, religion, sexual orientation, political
beliefs, immigration status, or similar traits for ordinary commercial discovery unless a specific lawful use case has been
separately approved with necessary consent/safeguards.

5. Signi cant Decisions
Influrios will perform required legal review before using automated processing to make decisions that produce legal or
similarly significant effects. Where applicable, required notices, opt-outs, access rights, human review, risk assessments, and
appeal mechanisms will be provided.

6. AI Providers and Data
Influrios may use multiple AI providers configured by function and country. Secrets must be secured and providers
contractually limited as appropriate. Personal data sent to AI providers should be minimized and governed by the Privacy
Policy and provider/data-processing terms.
