14. Age Eligibility & Minor Creator Policy
1. Launch Rule
Influrios commercial account registration, subscriptions, collaborations, payouts, business accounts, and creator Cards are
intended for users age 18 or older at launch, unless a separately approved minor/guardian program is activated in a jurisdiction.

2. Visitors
Public pages may be viewable by younger visitors, but Influrios is not designed as a children’s service and does not knowingly
solicit children under 13 to create accounts or provide personal information.

3. Minor Creator Program
A future minor creator program must address guardian consent and contracting authority, child labor/creator laws, trust/
earnings requirements where applicable, privacy/parental rights, mandated reporting obligations, and platform/paymentprovider age restrictions before activation.

4. Reports
If you believe a minor has created an unauthorized commercial account, contact [PRIVACY@INFLURIOS.COM] or
[SUPPORT@INFLURIOS.COM].
