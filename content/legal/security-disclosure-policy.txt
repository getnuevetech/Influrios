13. Security & Responsible Disclosure Policy
1. Reporting
Security researchers may report suspected vulnerabilities to [SECURITY@INFLURIOS.COM]. Include reproduction steps,
affected endpoint, impact, and any evidence needed to validate the issue.

2. Safe Research Expectations
•

Do not access, modify, or ex ltrate data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue.

•

Do not use social engineering, denial of service, malware, physical attacks, or credential theft.

•

Do not publicly disclose an unremediated issue before Influrios has a reasonable opportunity to investigate and x it.

•

Use test accounts/data where possible.

3. Good-Faith Handling
Influrios intends to evaluate good-faith reports, acknowledge valid ndings, and coordinate remediation. Any formal safeharbor commitment must be approved by counsel and re ected in the published version.
