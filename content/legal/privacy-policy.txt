1. Scope
This Privacy Policy applies to Influrios services operated by [OPERATING LEGAL ENTITY], including websites, apps,
Influencer Cards, public profiles, business tools, collaboration workflows, managed services, communications, and related
support. It does not replace privacy notices of third-party social networks, payment processors, or other integrated services.

2. Data We Collect
We may collect account and identity data; creator/business profile data; social account identifiers and authorized metrics;
public or licensed creator information used for unclaimed profiles; content/portfolio data; contact and inquiry data;
collaboration proposals, agreements, milestones and evidence; subscription and transaction metadata; device/log/security data;
approximate location derived from IP or user choice; communication preferences; support records; and AI-generated
classifications or recommendations. We aim to avoid collecting sensitive personal information unless required for a specific
lawful feature or provider process.

3. Sources
Sources may include you; users acting with authority for an organization; connected social/platform APIs; payment/KYC
providers; publicly available sources; licensed data providers; brand/agency submissions; cookies and analytics technologies;
security/fraud systems; and AI tools processing permitted inputs.

4. How We Use Data
We use data to provide and secure accounts; create and maintain profiles/Cards; verify identity or social ownership; power
search and matching; route inquiries; administer subscriptions; support collaboration agreements, funding, milestones and
disputes; personalize features; measure performance; detect abuse; communicate; comply with law/provider obligations;
conduct product analytics; and improve or develop features. Where required, we identify an appropriate legal basis such as
contract, consent, legitimate interests, or legal obligation.

5. AI, Profiling, and Automated Processing

assist search using automated systems. Creator-facing inferences should be editable or reviewable where practicable. Influrios

6. How We Share Data
We may share data with service providers/processors; payment and KYC providers; AI and analytics vendors; cloud/email/
security providers; counterparties in a collaboration where you direct or reasonably expect sharing; public visitors for profile
information you make public or information lawfully displayed on an unclaimed public profile; professional advisers;
authorities where legally required; and a successor in a corporate transaction. We do not sell private contact details as a
directory product merely because they are stored in an account.

7. Public Profiles and Cards
Information you choose to publish, or limited information displayed on a lawfully created unclaimed profile, may be publicly
accessible and indexed by search engines. Creators should use professional contact routes rather than private personal
information. Claim/correction/removal rights are described in separate policies.

8. Cookies and Tracking
Influrios uses necessary cookies and may use analytics, preference, advertising, or similar technologies subject to the Cookie
Policy and applicable consent/opt-out requirements. Where required, non-essential technologies are not activated until valid
consent is obtained.

9. Data Retention
We retain data for the period reasonably necessary for the purposes described, including account operation, collaboration/
payment records, fraud prevention, legal claims, tax/accounting, provider obligations, and consent evidence. Retention periods
vary by data type and jurisdiction. Deleted data may remain in backups for a limited period and in records we must retain by
law.

10. Security
Influrios uses administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the data, including access
controls, encryption in transit, secure secret storage, audit logging, and provider delegation for sensitive payment/KYC data
where possible. No system is completely secure.

11. Your Rights
Depending on location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy/port data, restrict or object to processing,
opt out of certain sale/sharing/targeted advertising/profiling, withdraw consent, appeal a privacy decision, or complain to a
regulator. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable rights. Identity verification may be required.

12. California and U.S. State Privacy
Where applicable, Influrios will provide required notices about categories, purposes, sharing, rights, appeal procedures,
sensitive data, targeted advertising, and profiling. If Influrios is determined to be a data broker in any jurisdiction, it will
address applicable registration and deletion-platform obligations before operating that model there.

13. EEA/UK and International Transfers
Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, Influrios will identify applicable legal bases, honor data-subject rights, and use an
appropriate transfer mechanism for international transfers when required, such as adequacy decisions or approved contractual
safeguards. Users may contact the privacy address below for more information.

14. Children
Commercial account registration is intended for adults 18+. Influrios does not knowingly operate a general commercial
creator-account service for children under 13. If we learn that personal information from a child was collected in violation of
applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete or otherwise address it.

15. Changes
We may update this Policy. Material changes will be communicated as required. The effective version and date will be
displayed.

16. Contact
Privacy inquiries and rights requests: [PRIVACY@INFLURIOS.COM]. Legal entity: [OPERATING LEGAL ENTITY].

17. Connected Social Accounts and API Data
When you connect a supported Social Platform account, Influrios may receive data made available by that platform within the
permissions/scopes you authorize. Depending on the platform and feature, this may include account identifiers, handles,
display name, profile image, bio, public content or content metadata, follower/subscriber counts, engagement and reach
metrics, audience or performance insights, connected business/ad account identifiers, campaign/reporting data, and
authorization metadata.
•

Influrios does not treat acceptance of Creator Terms as permission to access a third-party social account. Access is
initiated only through the applicable platform authorization or another lawful authorized connection method.

•

Influrios uses connected Social Platform data to provide requested features such as verification, Card/profile enrichment,
Influence Intelligence, search/matching, analytics, collaboration reporting, campaign administration, security/fraud
controls, and support.

•

Influrios seeks to request only permissions reasonably necessary for the enabled feature and may require reauthorization if
scopes or platform rules change.

•

Authentication/access tokens are treated as security credentials, are not displayed publicly, and should be stored with
appropriate encryption/secret-management controls. Influrios does not ask users to provide a Social Platform password
when an authorized OAuth or equivalent connection method exists.

•

Users may disconnect integrations or revoke platform permissions. Disconnection may stop future synchronization and
may remove or deprecate connected metrics, while historical collaboration, legal, security, consent, and accounting
records may be retained as permitted by law.
