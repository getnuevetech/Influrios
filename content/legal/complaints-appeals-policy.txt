2. Intake
Reports should identify the account/content/collaboration, explain the issue, and provide supporting evidence. Influrios may
prioritize urgent safety, fraud, privacy, and legal matters.

3. Enforcement Factors
Influrios may consider severity, intent, harm, prior violations, authenticity of evidence, legal obligations, provider
requirements, and whether a corrective action can adequately address the issue.

4. Appeals
Eligible users may submit one or more appeals as the product permits. Privacy-law appeal rights remain available where
required. Influrios may preserve or withhold investigative details to protect users, security systems, and legal obligations.

5. No Retaliation
Users must not retaliate against another user for making a good-faith report, exercising a privacy right, or making a legally
required sponsorship disclosure.
