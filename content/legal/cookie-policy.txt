2. Consent and Controls
Where law requires consent, Influrios will request it before activating non-essential technologies. Users may use the cookie/
settings interface to change available choices. Browser or device settings may provide additional controls.

3. Global Privacy Signals
Where legally required, Influrios will honor recognized opt-out preference signals for covered processing and will maintain
region-appropriate controls for targeted advertising, sale/sharing, or similar activities.

4. Third Parties
Third-party providers may set or receive identifiers under their own privacy terms. Influrios will maintain an internal inventory
of approved trackers and should not deploy new non-essential trackers outside the consent/configuration framework.

5. Retention
Cookie duration and related retention depend on purpose. Necessary security/session technologies should be limited to the
period needed; analytics/advertising identifiers should follow configured retention and regional requirements.
