7. Copyright & DMCA Policy
1. Respect for Copyright
Users may upload only content they own or are authorized to use. Influrios may remove or disable content that infringes
copyright or other intellectual-property rights.

2. DMCA Agent
Influrios intends to designate and register an agent with the U.S. Copyright Of ce where appropriate. Designated agent
deployment field: [DMCA AGENT NAME / ADDRESS / EMAIL]. This field must be completed and the registration
maintained before relying on the public DMCA process.

3. Takedown Notice Requirements
•

Signature of an authorized rights holder or agent

•

Identi cation of the copyrighted work

•

Identi cation and location of allegedly infringing material

•

Contact information for the complaining party

•

Good-faith statement that the use is not authorized

•

Accuracy/perjury statement regarding authority

4. Counter-Notice
A user whose content was removed may submit a legally sufficient counter-notice containing the information required by
applicable law. Influrios may forward the counter-notice to the complaining party and may restore material where the legal
process permits and no timely court action is received.

5. Repeat Infringers
Influrios may terminate repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances and may consider account history, valid notices, counternotices, and abuse of the notice process.

6. Other IP
Trademark, publicity, privacy, counterfeit, or other rights complaints that are not DMCA copyright notices should be submitted
through the general legal/support reporting channel.
