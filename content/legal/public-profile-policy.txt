3. Public Profile & Data Collection Policy
1. Why Influrios Creates Public Profiles
Influrios may create limited public creator profiles before a creator registers so businesses and users can discover creators by
specialty and so creators can later claim and enhance their professional presence.

2. Permitted Sources
•

Publicly available professional/creator information that may lawfully be used for this purpose

•

Of cial or authorized social APIs

•

Licensed data providers

•

Information submitted by the creator or an authorized manager/agency

•

Information supplied by a business or user where Influrios has a lawful basis to process it

Influrios should not design its core data model around circumventing access controls, violating platform terms, or collecting
private/sensitive information merely because it may be technically accessible.

3. What an Unclaimed Profile May Show
An unclaimed profile may show public creator name/handle, public profile image, professional location/market where
available, social links, public metrics, high-level specialties inferred from public content, and data-source/freshness indicators.
Private contact information should not be published without authorization.

4. AI Enrichment
Automated systems may propose specialties, topic concentration, content style, or match categories. These are inferences and
should be labeled or presented in a way that does not misstate them as creator-verified facts. Sensitive personal traits should not
be inferred for ordinary creator discovery.

5. Claim, Correction, and Removal
Creators may claim a profile, correct inaccurate data, dispute a profile, or request removal/opt-out under the applicable policy
and privacy law. Influrios may preserve limited suppression, fraud, legal, or audit records after removal where lawful.

6. Data Broker Review
Because laws can regulate businesses that collect and make available personal information about people with whom they lack a
direct relationship, Influrios must complete jurisdiction-specific legal review before activating the unclaimed-profile model in a
new market. Where registration or centralized deletion obligations apply, Influrios will not rely on this Policy as a substitute for
compliance.
