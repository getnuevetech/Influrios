6. Community Guidelines & Acceptable Use Policy
1. Prohibited Conduct
•

Impersonation, identity theft, fraudulent verification, or unauthorized account control

•

Harassment, threats, stalking, doxxing, hateful or unlawful abuse

•

Sexual exploitation, nonconsensual intimate imagery, or content sexualizing minors

•

Malware, phishing, credential theft, spam, automated abuse, scraping contrary to permission or law, or circumvention of
controls

•

Copyright/trademark/publicity/privacy infringement

•

Fake collaborations, fake brands, payment fraud, chargeback abuse, or counterfeit offers

•

Buying, selling, or knowingly using fake followers/views/engagement to misrepresent commercial in uence

•

Deceptive endorsements, fake testimonials, undisclosed sponsored content, or unsupported regulated-product claims

•

Illegal products/services, sanctions violations, or campaigns that payment providers or law prohibit

•

Using Influrios data to discriminate unlawfully or target people based on prohibited sensitive traits

2. Outreach Rules
Do not use Influrios to send deceptive, repetitive, or harassing solicitation. Respect do-not-contact, unsubscribe, suppression,
and channel-consent requirements. Referral or managed outreach must comply with applicable marketing rules.

3. Enforcement
Influrios may remove content, limit features, require verification, restrict outreach, suspend accounts, hold a payout where
legally/provider permitted, terminate access, or refer matters to providers or authorities. Severity, repetition, user safety, fraud,
legal obligations, and prior history may affect enforcement.

4. Appeals
Users may appeal eligible enforcement actions through the stated appeals process. Influrios may deny appeals or withhold
details where necessary to protect security, investigations, victims, or legal obligations.
