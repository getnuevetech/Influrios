12. Nonconsensual Intimate Imagery / Take It Down Policy
1. Zero Tolerance
Influrios prohibits nonconsensual intimate imagery, including qualifying authentic or AI-generated intimate depictions shared
without the depicted person’s consent, and any sexual content involving minors.

2. Removal Requests
Influrios will maintain a clear reporting channel for requests that meet applicable law. A valid request should identify the
depicted person or authorized representative, the material, its location, a statement of lack of consent, and other information
required by law. Where the Take It Down Act applies, Influrios will target removal of qualifying content and known identical
copies within the legally required period.

3. Preservation and Reporting
Influrios may preserve limited evidence when required or permitted for safety, legal process, fraud prevention, or lawenforcement reporting while restricting public access. Users must not re-upload removed material.

4. Minors
Any sexualized image of a minor, including AI-generated or manipulated material that violates law, is strictly prohibited and
may be escalated to appropriate reporting channels as required.

5. Contact
Report through the in-product safety form or [LEGAL@INFLURIOS.COM]/[SUPPORT@INFLURIOS.COM]. Counsel
should confirm the final Take It Down contact and operational SLA before launch.
