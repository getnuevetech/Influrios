INFLURIOS
Connected Social Data & API Policy
How Influrios handles data obtained through authorized social-platform integrations
Draft for Counsel Review | Version 1.1 | September 30, 2026

1. Purpose
This Policy explains how Influrios handles information obtained through an authorized connection to a Social Platform. It
supplements the Privacy Policy. It does not govern public-profile information collected independently from lawful public/licensed
sources except where those datasets are combined in a permitted Influrios feature.

2. Connected Data Categories
Depending on the Social Platform, approved product feature, and scopes authorized by the user, connected data may include account
IDs; usernames/handles; display names; profile images; bios; public profile links; content and content metadata; follower/subscriber
counts; reach, impressions, views, watch time, engagement and other performance metrics; audience insights; channel/page/account
analytics; business/ad/campaign identifiers; campaign reporting data; authorization scopes; token metadata; and connection status.

3. Authorization and Choice
Influrios obtains connected data through provider-approved APIs/SDKs or another lawful authorized method. A creator’s acceptance
of Influrios Creator Terms is not a substitute for Social Platform authorization. Users may be able to grant only a subset of requested
scopes and may revoke access later, depending on provider functionality.

4. Purposes
Connected data may be used for identity/account verification; creator profile/Card enrichment; Influence Intelligence; search and
matching; collaboration recommendations; agreed campaign measurement/reporting; creator analytics; fraud/security; support;
troubleshooting; and other user-requested functions described at collection.

5. Data Minimization and Scope Control
Influrios should request and process only data reasonably necessary for the enabled feature. New or materially broader data uses
should trigger appropriate product, privacy, and provider review and any additional consent required by law or provider policy.

Influrios should store Social Platform access/refresh tokens using secure secret-management or encrypted storage, restrict staff access
by role, log privileged actions, and never display access tokens in public/admin interfaces unnecessarily. Users should never be asked
to send Social Platform passwords by email, chat, or support ticket where an authorized connection method exists.

7. Public vs. Private Fields
Only data designated for public display by Influrios product settings and permitted by the Social Platform is shown on a public
profile/Card. Restricted insights, private campaign data, access tokens, private messages, and non-public business/ad data are not
treated as public profile fields.

8. Sharing
Influrios may share connected data with service providers that help operate the feature; with collaboration counterparties when the
user directs or reasonably expects the specific sharing; with authorities where legally required; or as otherwise described in the
Privacy Policy. Connected data is not made broadly available to unrelated businesses simply because the creator is searchable on
Influrios.

9. AI Processing
Influrios may submit minimized connected data to approved AI systems for permitted functions such as specialty analysis,
summaries, matching, reporting, fraud/security or support. Connected Social Platform API data should not be used to train a generalpurpose third-party AI model for the AI provider’s independent benefit unless the Social Platform rules, law, and any required user
authorization permit that use.

10. Retention and Refresh
Retention depends on the data type, feature, provider rules, legal obligations, and user settings. Influrios may refresh connected
metrics while authorization remains active. Where a Social Platform requires periodic refresh, deletion, reauthorization, or restricted
caching, Influrios will design the integration to comply with those requirements.

11. Disconnection and Deletion
When a user disconnects or revokes an integration, Influrios should stop future data collection from that connection after technical
processing completes. Influrios may delete, de-identify, expire, or retain existing data according to provider rules, the Privacy Policy,
legal obligations, contracts, fraud/security needs, and accounting/payment retention requirements. Users may submit deletion
requests through [PRIVACY@INFLURIOS.COM] or an available self-service control.

12. Business Integrations
Business Social Platform integrations are optional by default and ordinarily enabled for specific collaboration, campaign, reporting,
advertising, or analytics functions. Private business/ad account data is compartmentalized according to account permissions and
collaboration access rules.

13. Platform Changes and Availability
Social Platforms control their APIs and may modify scopes, quotas, terms, app-review requirements, retention rules, or data
availability. Influrios may adjust or discontinue an integration to remain compliant or operational.
