5. Influrios Card Terms
1. Product
The Influrios Card is a vertical digital creator business card linked to a public online profile. It may include creator identity,
image, specialty, location, social links, contact routes, shortlinks, QR codes, analytics, media-kit indicators, and collaboration
actions according to plan entitlements.

2. Plan Entitlements
Feature availability is determined by the live admin-managed entitlement matrix, not by hard-coded plan names. Current
launch defaults may include Starter with a standard public link and limited social presence, Plus with expanded social links/
standard QR/shortlink, and Pro with dynamic QR and advanced professional features. Influrios may modify future entitlements
with applicable notice.

3. QR and Shortlinks
A QR or shortlink is a routing tool, not a certi cation of the linked person. Dynamic QR destinations may be updated without
changing the printed code. Influrios may suspend or reroute links for security, legal, impersonation, or policy reasons. Users
must not use Cards to redirect visitors to malicious, deceptive, illegal, or unrelated content.

4. Branding
Starter and other tiers may display Influrios branding. Where a plan permits customization, creator themes must preserve
required verification, safety, legal, and accessibility elements. A creator cannot alter a badge or interface to falsely imply
endorsement, verified metrics, or a higher subscription tier.

5. Analytics
Card views, QR scans, clicks, and approximate location analytics are estimates subject to technical limitations, privacy
controls, bot ltering, and platform settings. They are not audited advertising measurements unless expressly identified as such.

6. Public Information
Creators should publish professional information only. Do not place sensitive personal information, private addresses,
authentication credentials, nancial account information, or other high-risk data on a public Card.
