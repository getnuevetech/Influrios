1. Scope and Incorporation
These Social Platform Integration & Connected Accounts Terms (“Integration Terms”) supplement the Influrios Terms of Service,
Creator Terms, Business Terms, Privacy Policy, and other applicable agreements. They govern any feature that connects Influrios to a
third-party social network, creator platform, advertising/business manager, analytics service, or related API (“Social Platform”).

2. Creator Acceptance
Every creator/in uencer who registers or claims a creator profile on Influrios accepts these Integration Terms as part of the creator
legal framework, even if the creator does not immediately connect a Social Platform account. This establishes the rules for future
connections. It does not itself grant Influrios access to a third-party account.

3. Business Acceptance
Businesses and brands are not required to connect Social Platform accounts merely to register, search, or use ordinary business
features. These Integration Terms apply to a business when it elects or is required for a collaboration to connect an authorized social,
advertising, analytics, business-manager, or campaign account.

4. Separate Platform Authorization
A Social Platform connection is created only after the user completes the authorization method required by that Social Platform or
another approved lawful process. Where OAuth/scoped permissions are available, Influrios will rely on that mechanism rather than
asking the user to disclose the Social Platform password. The Social Platform may allow the user to grant, decline, or revoke
particular permissions.

5. Authority to Connect
You represent that you own, control, or are authorized to administer every Social Platform account you connect. You may not connect
another person’s or organization’s account without authority. Agencies/managers may be required to obtain creator confirmation.

Influrios may request only permissions reasonably related to an enabled feature. Requested scopes may include access to profile
identity, public content/content metadata, audience/performance metrics, channel/account statistics, campaign/ad reporting, or other
data supported by the Social Platform. The specific scopes available depend on the provider and may change.

7. Permitted Uses
Influrios may use connected-account data to verify account ownership/control; populate or refresh an Influrios Card/profile; calculate
or display permitted metrics; provide Influence Intelligence; improve search/matching; administer collaborations; produce agreed
campaign reports; detect fraud/security issues; provide support; and perform other functions disclosed in the connection flow or
Privacy Policy.

8. Public Display
Connection does not make all imported data public. Influrios will determine public display according to the creator’s settings, plan
entitlements, product design, applicable Social Platform rules, and law. Access tokens, private account credentials, and restricted
analytics are not public profile fields.

9. Creator Collaboration Reporting
If a collaboration requires creator-side Social Platform reporting, the collaboration should identify the material reporting expectations
before acceptance. Influrios may use connected metrics to verify or summarize agreed deliverables and performance where
permitted.

10. Business Collaboration Integration
A business may connect authorized business/ad/campaign accounts for campaign creation, ownership verification, reporting, or
measurement. Influrios will not expose one business’s private connected-account information to another business merely because
both express interest in the same creator or opportunity.

11. Revocation, Disconnection, and Expiration
Users may disconnect supported integrations or revoke access through Influrios and/or the Social Platform. Revocation generally
stops future synchronization after processing completes, but does not require deletion of records Influrios must or may retain for
contracts, payments, disputes, fraud prevention, consent evidence, tax/accounting, security, or legal obligations. Some features may
become unavailable after disconnection.

12. Metrics and Availability
Third-party metrics may be delayed, estimated, rounded, incomplete, changed, or unavailable. Influrios may show data-source and
freshness indicators. Social Platforms may change APIs, scopes, quotas, review rules, or access at any time, and Influrios does not
guarantee continuous availability.

13. Tokens and Security
Influrios treats access/refresh tokens, app credentials, and similar connection secrets as sensitive credentials. Users must not publish
or share them. Influrios may invalidate a connection, require reauthorization, or suspend integrations where compromise or
unauthorized access is suspected.

Influrios will provide available mechanisms to disconnect accounts and request deletion/correction of personal data, subject to legal
