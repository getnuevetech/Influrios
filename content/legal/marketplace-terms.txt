7. Collaboration Marketplace Terms
1. Scope
These terms govern collaboration proposals, matching, opportunity posts, invitations, deal workspaces, fee calculations,
milestone tracking, and related communications. A per-deal Creator-Brand or Creator-Creator Agreement controls the
commercial details of an accepted collaboration.

2. Service Levels
Influrios may support discovery, platform match, contracted collaboration, managed introduction, and managed campaign
services. Availability and fees depend on country, plan, collaboration type, and legal/provider enablement.

3. Fee Matrix
Fees may be percentage-based, at, tiered, minimum/maximum, waived, or enterprise-custom. Fees may be charged to the
creator, business, or both. The applicable fee is displayed before acceptance and stored as an immutable snapshot for the deal.
Future fee-rule changes do not silently change an accepted deal.

4. Relationship Attribution
Influrios may apply a disclosed attribution period to relationships introduced through the platform or managed outreach. The
period must not be inde nite. Users may declare a documented pre-existing relationship before acceptance. Disputed
attribution may be reviewed by Influrios.

5. Off-Platform Circumvention
Users must not intentionally move an Influrios-introduced transaction off-platform solely to evade an accepted platform fee or
protected-payment obligation during the applicable attribution period. This does not give Influrios ownership of a creator’s
career or unrelated future relationships.

6. Changes
Material changes to compensation, deliverables, milestones, usage rights, or deadlines after acceptance should be documented
in a change order accepted by affected parties.
