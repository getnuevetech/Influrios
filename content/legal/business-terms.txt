INFLURIOS

Business / Brand Terms
Supplemental terms for brands, businesses, buyers, and campaign sponsors
Draft for Counsel Review | Version 1.1 | September 30, 2026
IMPORTANT: This is a business/legal drafting template, not a substitute for advice from licensed counsel. Complete all bracketed
deployment fields, confirm the operating entity and jurisdictions, and obtain counsel review before publication or user acceptance.

1. Authority
You represent that you are authorized to act for the business account, create opportunities, approve budgets, enter
collaborations, and grant any product/content materials you provide.

2. Creator Discovery and Data
Creator profiles, metrics, AI t explanations, and public/unclaimed information are discovery tools, not guarantees. You must
perform appropriate due diligence and may not use Influrios data to make unlawful discriminatory decisions or infer sensitive
traits unrelated to a legitimate campaign purpose.

3. Campaign Briefs and Claims
Campaign briefs must accurately identify the brand/product, material requirements, eligibility restrictions, claims that may be
made, compensation, usage rights, disclosure requirements, deadlines, and any regulated-product restrictions. You are
responsible for substantiating advertising/product claims you ask creators to communicate.

4. Creator Compensation and Scope
You must honor accepted compensation, milestones, revision limits, cancellation/kill fees, and usage rights. You may not use
acceptance of a deliverable as a blanket transfer of unrelated intellectual-property, publicity, or AI/synthetic rights.

5. Advertising Compliance
Brands must provide creators with disclosure guidance, must not instruct creators to conceal material relationships, and should
reasonably monitor campaigns for compliance. You must not procure fake testimonials or knowingly purchase/distribute fake
social in uence indicators.

6. Protected Funding
Where protected payment is enabled, you authorize the approved payment provider and Influrios workflow to collect/fund the
agreed amount, release amounts by accepted milestones, apply disclosed fees, and process refunds/disputes under the
applicable policy. Provider KYC, sanctions, payment, and chargeback rules apply.

7. Use of Creator Content
You receive only the license stated in the collaboration agreement. Paid media, whitelisting, perpetual use, sublicensing, outof-home, TV, AI training, synthetic reproduction, and similar rights are not implied merely because a creator was paid.

8. Fair Dealing
Businesses may not retaliate against creators for lawful disclosures, refuse payment for reasons outside the agreed acceptance
criteria, demand unlimited revisions, threaten unsupported legal action to suppress legitimate feedback, or circumvent
platform protections in breach of an accepted agreement.

9. Optional Business Social and Campaign Integrations
A business/brand account is not required to connect a Social Platform, advertising account, analytics account, or campaign
account merely to register or browse Influrios. A connection may be offered or required for a specific collaboration, campaignmanagement feature, reporting workflow, or other business-selected functionality.
•

You may connect only business/social/ad accounts that you are authorized to administer.

•

Before connection, Influrios will identify the integration and the authorization/scopes requested through the applicable
provider flow or product notice.

•

Connected business data may be used to create or manage collaboration briefs, verify campaign ownership, measure
agreed performance, reconcile campaign reporting, or provide another enabled feature disclosed to you.

•

Influrios will not make one business’s private connected-account data available to competing businesses merely because
they are participating in the same creator opportunity.

•

If a collaboration requires creator-side or business-side social reporting, the relevant parties should be told what reporting
is required before accepting the collaboration.

•

You may revoke a connection, but revocation does not erase already executed contracts, payment records, legally required
audit evidence, or agreed campaign reports that Influrios is entitled or required to retain.
