1. Scope and Priority
These Creator Terms supplement the Terms of Service. If a con ict exists, a signed collaboration-specific agreement controls
that collaboration, followed by these Creator Terms, then the general Terms to the extent of the con ict.

2. Creator Profile and Card
•

You control creator-supplied profile fields and must keep them accurate.

•

You may claim an unclaimed profile only if you are the depicted creator or are authorized to act for that creator.

•

You may connect only social accounts you control or are authorized to manage.

•

Starter/Plus/Pro entitlements are controlled by the live plan matrix and may vary by jurisdiction.

•

Your public Card/Profile may be indexed or shared publicly according to your settings and the public-profile policies.

3. Metrics and Authenticity
You must not knowingly buy, fabricate, manipulate, or misrepresent followers, views, engagement, testimonials, verification,
audience data, or other indicators of in uence for a commercial purpose. Influrios may label data source/freshness and may
suspend features or require review where anomalies suggest fraud or manipulation.

4. Creator Content and Rights
You represent that content you upload or license through Influrios is owned by you or used with sufficient permission. You
retain ownership except to the extent a specific collaboration agreement grants a de ned license. You must obtain permissions
for third-party music, footage, images, trademarks, locations, models, and other protected material where legally required.

5. Endorsements and Product Claims
When content is sponsored, gifted, commissioned, or otherwise involves a material connection, you must make clear and
conspicuous disclosures and must not make claims you know are false, misleading, or unsupported. You may not claim
personal experience with a product you did not actually use where such a claim would be deceptive.

6. Collaboration Protections
•

Scope, deliverables, deadlines, revision rounds, compensation, usage rights, exclusivity, cancellation/kill fees, and
milestones should be xed in the collaboration agreement before work begins.

•

You are not required to transfer perpetual ownership or unrestricted likeness rights unless expressly agreed.

•

Brand use of your name, image, voice, content, or AI/synthetic replica must stay within the license actually granted.

•

Where protected funding is enabled, do not begin protected work until the system indicates the required funding condition
is satis ed unless you voluntarily assume the risk outside that protection.

7. Taxes and Independent Business Status
Unless a separate lawful relationship states otherwise, creators act as independent businesses and are responsible for taxes,
permits, insurance, and business obligations arising from their earnings. Influrios may collect tax information or report
payments when legally required.

8. Managed Promotion
Influrios will not act as your exclusive agent or bind you to a deal unless a separately signed agreement expressly and lawfully
grants that authority. Managed introductions or negotiation may be unavailable in jurisdictions where licensing or other legal
requirements have not been satis ed.

9. Contact and Privacy
Use professional contact routes for public display. Influrios may route inquiries without revealing private phone/email
information unless you choose to publish or disclose it.

10. Social Platform Integration and Creator Authorization
These social-integration provisions apply to every Influrios creator/in uencer account as part of the Creator Terms, even if the
creator has not yet connected a Social Platform account. They establish the contractual rules for any current or future
connection. They do not by themselves authorize Influrios to access a third-party account.
•

You may connect only a Social Platform account that you own or are authorized to manage for the creator represented on
Influrios.

•

When connecting an account, you authorize Influrios to receive and use only the data and permissions made available
through the scopes/authorization you approve and for the purposes disclosed in the applicable ow, these Terms, and the
Privacy Policy.

•

You remain responsible for complying with the Social Platform’s own terms, creator rules, music/content restrictions,
advertising rules, and account requirements.

•

You may revoke or disconnect a supported integration. Doing so may disable verification, synchronized metrics, Card
features, analytics, matching inputs, or campaign reporting that depend on the connection.

•

You authorize Influrios to refresh permitted data while the connection remains active, subject to platform rules, plan
entitlements, product settings, and privacy law.
