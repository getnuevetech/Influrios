4. Profile Claim, Correction, Opt-Out & Removal Policy
1. Claiming a Profile
A creator or authorized representative may request control of an unclaimed profile. Influrios may require proof such as socialaccount authentication, email/domain control, government/identity verification through an approved provider, or other
reasonable evidence.

2. Correction
Any person may report materially inaccurate public profile information. Influrios may request supporting evidence and may
prioritize identity, impersonation, contact, and harmful inaccuracies.

3. Removal / Opt-Out
A creator may request removal or suppression of an unclaimed profile where supported by policy or applicable law. Removal
may not erase transaction, consent, dispute, fraud, or legal records Influrios must retain. A suppression record may be kept to
prevent the profile from being automatically recreated.

4. Unauthorized Claim or Impersonation
Influrios may reverse a claim, lock a profile, request re-verification, or preserve evidence when ownership is disputed.
Submitting fraudulent identity or authorization documents may result in termination and referral where appropriate.

5. Appeals
A person whose claim, correction, or removal request is denied may appeal using the stated support/privacy route. Privacy-law
appeal rights take precedence where applicable.

6. Request Channels
Use the in-product profile controls where available or contact [PRIVACY@INFLURIOS.COM] /
[SUPPORT@INFLURIOS.COM]. Influrios may require sufficient information to locate the profile and verify the request.
